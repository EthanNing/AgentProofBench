Reading the measurements +

The empirical comparisons use DeepSeek Flash and Pro with thinking disabled. Each reuse family contributes one related transformation and one replay witness. The panel–delivery association is an exploratory analysis of 44 assignments clustered within 22 families.

Scores follow the explicit reference predicates. A separate GPT-6 Astra LLM-as-a-judge audit reviews 195 states across 62 tasks, agreeing with 191 of 192 resolved labels (99.5%); three cases remain indeterminate. It corroborates all 48 requirement changes, all 48 failed replays, and both delivered-regression pairs. Inspect the audit. The paper preserves original labels, post-inspection exclusions, and the exact feedback policies.
